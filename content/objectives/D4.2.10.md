## Azure first

- **Microsoft Purview Audit** is the Microsoft 365 *unified audit log*: one record each time a user or admin does something in Exchange, SharePoint, OneDrive, Teams, Entra ID, Defender and more. It lives in the Microsoft 365 tenant, not in Azure.
- **Audit (Standard)** comes with most Microsoft 365 plans. **Audit (Premium)** comes with E5-level licences and adds longer retention.
- The **Defender portal** (`security.microsoft.com`) can search that same log. It also has **advanced hunting**, the KQL screen you used as an analyst.
- **Microsoft Sentinel** can copy Microsoft 365 audit records into its Log Analytics workspace with the **Microsoft 365** connector. They land in the `OfficeActivity` table.

**Licence-blocked:** this needs a Microsoft 365 tenant with auditing on (and E5 for Premium). Treat this page as a portal tour.

See the Foundations pages listed above: Purview basics, Defender portal tour, Microsoft 365 tenants and licensing, Log Analytics workspace.

## In one sentence

You query Purview Audit from the Defender portal in two ways: the **Audit** page (a form-based search, identical to the one in the Purview portal), or **advanced hunting** with KQL against `CloudAppEvents` (fed by Defender for Cloud Apps) and, once Sentinel is onboarded, `OfficeActivity` (fed by the Sentinel Microsoft 365 connector).

## What's new for an engineer

As an analyst you ran the query. Now you make sure the data exists, is kept long enough, and is readable by the right people:

1. **Auditing is on.** Unified audit logging is on by default for most tenants. Check it in Purview > **Audit** or with Exchange Online PowerShell (`Get-AdminAuditLogConfig`, property `UnifiedAuditLogIngestionEnabled`).
2. **Retention is a licence decision.** Audit (Standard) keeps records **180 days**. Audit (Premium) keeps Entra ID, Exchange, OneDrive and SharePoint records of E5-licensed users for **1 year** by default. Other records stay 180 days unless an **audit log retention policy** extends them. With the **10-Year Audit Log Retention** add-on, policies go to **10 years**.
3. **Feeds for KQL.** `CloudAppEvents` fills only if Defender for Cloud Apps has the Microsoft 365 app connector with **Microsoft 365 activities** ticked. `OfficeActivity` fills only if you enable the Sentinel **Microsoft 365** connector (Exchange, SharePoint, Teams). `OfficeActivity` ingestion is **free**. Retention beyond 90 days is not.
4. **Roles.** They are assigned in three different places. See the table below.

## What actually matters

| Task | Role needed | Where it is assigned |
|---|---|---|
| Search the audit log (Defender or Purview **Audit** page) | **Audit Logs** or **View-Only Audit Logs** role (in **Organization Management** / **Compliance Management** role groups); Entra **Compliance Administrator** also works | Purview portal or Exchange admin center |
| Create audit log retention policies | **Organization Configuration** role; max **50** policies | Purview portal |
| Enable the Sentinel Microsoft 365 connector | Read/write on the workspace (**Microsoft Sentinel Contributor**) + Entra **Security Administrator** (or Global Administrator) | Azure RBAC + Entra ID |
| Query Sentinel tables (`OfficeActivity`) in advanced hunting | At least **Microsoft Sentinel Reader** | Azure RBAC |

| Route | Data source | Retention you get | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defender portal **Audit** page | Purview unified audit log | Purview: 180 days / 1 year / up to 10 years | Compliance search and CSV export, no KQL |
| Advanced hunting `CloudAppEvents` | Defender for Cloud Apps | Advanced hunting default 30 days | Joins with device, email and identity tables |
| Advanced hunting `OfficeActivity` | Sentinel Microsoft 365 connector | Your Sentinel table settings (90 days free, up to 12 years total) | Long-term KQL, analytics rules, incidents |

- Audit search runs as **search jobs**: up to **10** at once per user, kept for **30 days**, export up to **50,000** rows (Standard) or **1,000,000** rows (Premium).
- Audit (Standard) used to keep **90 days**. Records created before **October 17, 2023** still follow that older value.
- Records from non-user entities (service principals, system events) are kept a fixed **1 year**. Custom policies do not apply to them.

```kql
// Advanced hunting: SharePoint downloads by one user (Sentinel table)
OfficeActivity
| where TimeGenerated > ago(90d)
| where RecordType == "SharePointFileOperation" and Operation == "FileDownloaded"
| where UserId =~ "user@contoso.com"
| project TimeGenerated, UserId, ClientIP, Site_Url, SourceFileName

// Same idea from Defender for Cloud Apps data (30 days in advanced hunting)
CloudAppEvents
| where Timestamp > ago(30d)
| where ActionType == "FileDownloaded"
| project Timestamp, AccountDisplayName, IPAddress, Application, ObjectName
```

> **Training module and product docs disagree.** Unit 4 says the Purview Audit / Microsoft 365 connector brings in both `OfficeActivity` and `CopilotActivity`. The Sentinel tables reference lists `CopilotActivity` under a separate **Microsoft Copilot** connector, and `OfficeActivity` under **Microsoft 365 (formerly, Office 365)**. The unit also leaves "[REVIEW]" notes about the connector name. Go with the product docs: two connectors, two tables.

## Where it lives

- **Defender portal — Audit search:** `Microsoft Defender portal > Audit` (direct link `https://security.microsoft.com/auditlogsearch`) → **New Search** → dates, users, activities, workload → **Search** → **Export**. Same tool as `Microsoft Purview portal > Audit`.
- **Defender portal — KQL:** `Microsoft Defender portal > Investigation & response > Hunting > Advanced hunting`. Sentinel tables appear under the **Schema** tab after the Defender tables.
- **CloudAppEvents feed:** `Microsoft Defender portal > Settings > Cloud apps > App connectors` → Microsoft 365 → tick **Microsoft 365 activities**.
- **OfficeActivity feed:** `Microsoft Defender portal > Microsoft Sentinel > Configuration > Data connectors` → **Microsoft 365** → tick Exchange / SharePoint / Teams → **Apply Changes**.
- **Retention policies:** `Microsoft Purview portal > Audit > Policies` → **Create audit retention policy**.

## Exam traps

- ==!Defender's Audit page has its own copy and retention.== No. It is the **same** Purview audit log. Retention follows Purview licences and policies.
- ==!A Defender XDR or Sentinel role lets you search the audit log.== The Audit page needs **Audit Logs / View-Only Audit Logs** (Purview or Exchange role groups), not Security Reader.
- ==!`CloudAppEvents` is always populated.== Only when **Defender for Cloud Apps** has the Microsoft 365 connector with *Microsoft 365 activities* on.
- ==!Audit (Standard) keeps 90 days.== It is **180 days** for records since October 17, 2023.
- ==!Premium keeps everything for a year.== Only Entra ID, Exchange, OneDrive and SharePoint records of E5-licensed users. Others stay 180 days unless a policy changes it.
- ==!10-year retention is retroactive.== It is not. It starts from when the policy and add-on are in place.
- Need Microsoft 365 audit records for **longer than a year, queryable with KQL and alertable** → ==+Sentinel Microsoft 365 connector into `OfficeActivity`, with total retention set==.

## Skip

- **Skip:** unit 5's `CopilotActivity` query with columns `AppContext`, `PromptType` and `ResponseSummary`. Those columns do not appear in the published `CopilotActivity` schema. Know only that the table exists and comes from the Microsoft Copilot connector.
- **Skip:** the Office 365 Management Activity API bandwidth numbers and the `Search-UnifiedAuditLog` script. Know that both exist.
- **Skip:** the full audit activity catalogue (thousands of operations). Know the common ones: `FileDownloaded`, `MailItemsAccessed` (Premium), `FileAccessed`.
- **Skip:** per-workload "intelligent insights" detail. One line: Premium adds events such as `MailItemsAccessed`.
- **Skip:** the "about 30 minutes" ingestion lag claimed in unit 4. Product docs do not give one figure.
