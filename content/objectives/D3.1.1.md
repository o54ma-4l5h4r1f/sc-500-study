## Azure first

- A **SharePoint site** is a shared web space for files, owned by a team. Its **permissions** say who can open what. **Sharing links** ("Anyone", "People in your organization", "Specific people") open single files to more people.
- **Everyone except external users (EEEU)** is a built-in SharePoint group that contains every internal account. Granting it access makes content readable by the whole company.
- **Microsoft 365 Copilot** answers prompts by searching content the signed-in user can already open (this is called **grounding**). It never breaks permissions. It just finds what permissions already allow — fast.
- **Sensitivity labels** (Microsoft Purview) are tags like "Confidential" stamped on files. **DLP** (data loss prevention) policies act on those labels.
- **Licence:** everything here is `license_blocked` for you. You need a Microsoft 365 tenant with Microsoft 365 Copilot licences (which switch on SharePoint Advanced Management) and Purview DSPM. Treat the screenshots as the lab.

New to these? Read the Foundations pages listed in "Before you start".

## In one sentence

Overexposure means a SharePoint site's audience is far wider than its content deserves, and you find it with two tool sets: SharePoint Advanced Management reports in the SharePoint admin center, and data risk assessments in Microsoft Purview DSPM.

## Why it exists

In SOC work you investigated data leaks after they happened. Copilot changes the timing. A finance file shared with EEEU three years ago sat unnoticed because nobody searched for it. Now a junior analyst types "summarize the Q3 earnings forecast" and Copilot finds it in seconds. Nothing was hacked. Permissions were simply wrong, and AI made that visible.

Think of an office where the HR filing cabinet was left unlocked for years. Nobody wandered in. Then the company hired a very fast assistant who answers any question by opening every unlocked drawer. The fix is not to fire the assistant. It is to find the unlocked drawers.

## What actually matters

**1. Two discovery tools, two portals.**

| | SharePoint data access governance (DAG) reports | Purview data risk assessments |
|---|---|---|
| Portal | ==@SharePoint admin center== > Reports > Data access governance | ==@Purview portal== > DSPM > Discover > Data risk assessments |
| Scope | every SharePoint and OneDrive site | default: ==@top 100 SharePoint sites by usage, weekly==; custom: sites/users you pick |
| Sees file contents? | no — permission and sharing metadata | yes — scans for sensitive information types and labels |
| Remediation | RAC, site access review, change history | DLP by label, RCD, auto-labeling, retention (from the **Protect** tab) |
| Role | SharePoint Administrator (or SharePoint Advanced Management Administrator) | Compliance Administrator |

**2. The DAG reports.** **Snapshot** reports show the current state: *site permissions across your organization* (the "oversharing baseline"), *site permissions for users* (everything one user can open), and *sensitivity labels applied to files* (that one also needs E5). **Activity** reports cover the last ==@28 days==: *sharing links* (Anyone, People in your organization, Specific people) and *shared with EEEU*. Microsoft suggests snapshots quarterly and activity reports monthly. Without SharePoint Advanced Management, an E5 admin gets only activity reports (up to 10,000 sites, ==!no snapshots, no remediation==) after enabling data collection. Reports appear 24 hours later, data is kept 28 days, and collection pauses after 3 months without a report.

**3. Site access review — make the owner fix it.** Admins can't see item-level details for compliance reasons, so you delegate. From a DAG report, select up to ==@100 sites== in the web view (PowerShell for more) and choose **Initiate site access review**. Owners get an email, fix access, then select **Complete review**. Track status on the **My review requests** tab. You can start up to 1,000 reviews per calendar month from the site permissions report. OneDrive isn't supported.

**4. Restricted content discovery (RCD) — hide, don't lock.** A site-level setting. Content stops appearing in organization-wide search and Copilot. Copilot buttons and "Create pages with AI" disappear on that site. ==!Permissions don't change== — people with access still open files directly. Content stays in the index, so eDiscovery and auto-labeling still work. Microsoft calls it a ==@temporary== control while you right-size access. Sites over 500,000 items can take more than a week to update. SharePoint sites only, not OneDrive.

**5. Restricted access control (RAC) — lock the door.** Only members of up to ==@10== Microsoft 365 or Entra security groups (dynamic groups allowed) can open the site, even if they hold a direct permission or a sharing link. Search and Copilot honor it. Being in the group ==!doesn't grant access== — a user needs both group membership and a permission. Turn it on tenant-wide first (`Policies > Access control > Site-level access restriction`); it can take an hour. Works on OneDrive too. Shared and private channel sites need their own policy.

**6. Purview data risk assessment flyout.** Per site: **Overview** (items scanned, sensitive items, Anyone links), **Identify** (scanned vs not scanned, start an on-demand classification scan), **Protect** (actions below), **Monitor** (shared with anyone / everyone / specific people / externally, plus **Start a SharePoint site access review**). Protect actions: **Restrict access by label** (DLP policy that stops Microsoft 365 Copilot and agents summarizing items with chosen labels), **Restrict all items** (RCD), **Create an auto-labeling policy**, **Create retention policies** (content not accessed for at least ==@3 years==). The first default assessment shows results after a ==@4-day== delay. Custom assessments: wait 48 hours, results expire after 30 days, limit 200,000 items per location. Optional **item-level** scanning needs an Entra app registration and covers up to 10 SharePoint sites (no OneDrive); actions are Resolve, Apply sensitivity label, Notify owner, Remove sharing link.

## Where it lives

| Task | Portal and path | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Run DAG reports, start site access reviews | SharePoint admin center > Reports > Data access governance | SharePoint Administrator |
| Turn on RCD for a site | SharePoint admin center > Sites > Active sites > *site* > Settings > **Restrict content from Microsoft Copilot** | SharePoint Administrator |
| Allow RAC tenant-wide | SharePoint admin center > Policies > Access control > Site-level access restriction | SharePoint Administrator |
| Data risk assessments | Microsoft Purview portal > DSPM > Discover > Data risk assessments | Compliance Administrator |

```powershell
# Restricted content discovery (hide from Copilot and org-wide search)
Set-SPOSite -Identity https://contoso.sharepoint.com/sites/finance -RestrictContentOrgWideSearch $true
# Restricted access control (only listed groups get in)
Set-SPOTenant -EnableRestrictedAccessControl $true
Set-SPOSite -Identity https://contoso.sharepoint.com/sites/finance -RestrictedAccessControl $true
Set-SPOSite -Identity https://contoso.sharepoint.com/sites/finance -AddRestrictedAccessControlGroups <groupGuid>
```

Licensing: a Microsoft 365 base licence (E3/E5/A5, E1, or government) plus **either** one Microsoft 365 Copilot licence assigned to any user **or** the SharePoint Advanced Management Plan 1 add-on, **or** Microsoft 365 E7. The sensitivity-label report also needs E5.

## Exam traps

- **"Hide a site from Copilot without changing anyone's access"** → ==+restricted content discovery==. Not RAC, which ==!blocks people too==.
- **"Only the Finance group may open this site, even with old sharing links"** → ==+restricted access control==.
- **"Copilot respects permissions, so oversharing isn't a Copilot risk."** ==!False.== Copilot respects permissions — that's exactly why bad permissions leak.
- **RCD removes content from the index.** ==!No.== eDiscovery and auto-labeling keep working.
- **"Stop Copilot summarizing Highly Confidential files but keep the site permissions"** → ==+DLP policy for Microsoft 365 Copilot (restrict access by label)==.
- **Who fixes item-level access?** The ==+site owner== via a site access review — the admin can't see item details.
- **Default data risk assessment** = top ==@100== sites, weekly, no setup. Not "all sites".
- **Portal confusion:** DAG reports are in the ==@SharePoint admin center==; data risk assessments are in the ==@Purview portal==.

> **Contradiction — training module vs product docs.** The module (written for **DSPM for AI (classic)**, `Solutions > DSPM for AI (classic)`) puts data risk assessments there. The current product moves them to **DSPM > Discover > Data risk assessments** and surfaces them under the objective **Prevent oversharing of sensitive data**. The flyout tabs and Protect actions are identical. The module also suggests "conditional access policies that restrict Copilot's ability to reference that site" — Conditional Access can protect a *site* through an **authentication context** (a SharePoint Advanced Management feature), but that gates people at sign-in, not Copilot's referencing. The Copilot-specific per-site controls are RCD and RAC, plus DLP by label. The UI toggle is named **Restrict content from Microsoft Copilot**; some docs still call it "Restrict content discovery".

## Skip

- **Skip:** the module's 24-hour / one-week / monitor priority table. It's the Contoso story, not a product setting.
- **Skip:** the Fabric tab of data risk assessments (Entra app, Fabric admin API settings, item types). Out of scope for a SharePoint bullet.
- **Skip:** RAC insight cmdlets (`Start-SPORestrictedAccessForSitesInsights`), the "Learn more" error-link setting and the sharing-outside-group opt-in. Know RAC exists and what it blocks.
- **Skip:** the Graph permission list for the item-level scanning app. Know it needs an Entra app with admin consent.
- **Skip:** the "permissioned users" counting rules in the owner's review page (no de-duplication). Operational detail.
- **Skip:** email customization for site access reviews. Know owners get an email.
- **Skip:** **Restricted SharePoint Search** (an allow list of up to 100 sites). It is retiring — ==!new enablement blocked from 31 July 2026== — and RCD replaces it. If a distractor offers it, prefer RCD.
- **Skip:** SharePoint Admin Agent, AI insights and Content Management Assessment. Newer helpers on top of the same reports; the bullet tests the reports and controls.
