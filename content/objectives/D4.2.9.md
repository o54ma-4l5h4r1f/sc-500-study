## Azure first

- A **Log Analytics workspace** is the Azure database Sentinel sits on. Every log type lands in its own **table** (`SecurityEvent`, `SigninLogs`, `OfficeActivity`).
- Retention is set **per table**. A workspace-wide default covers every table you have not overridden.
- Azure Monitor gives each table a **plan**: *Analytics* (full KQL, alerts), *Basic* (cheap, limited queries) or *Auxiliary* (cheapest, slow queries).
- The **Microsoft Sentinel data lake** is a low-cost store, billed separately, that sits behind the workspace. Analytics data is mirrored into it. It replaces what older docs call "archive".
- You change these settings as an Azure resource change. That needs an Azure RBAC role on the workspace, such as **Log Analytics Contributor**.

See the Foundations pages listed above: Log Analytics workspace, Defender portal tour, Azure RBAC and Entra roles.

## In one sentence

Retention in Sentinel is two numbers per table: **analytics retention** (how long data stays hot and usable by rules and hunting) and **total retention** (how long it exists at all, with the extra time held cheaply in the data lake tier). Up to 2 years for the first number, up to 12 years for the second.

## What's new for an engineer

As an analyst you met retention as "nothing older than 90 days". Now you set it. Four decisions:

1. **Tier per table.** Detection tables stay in the **Analytics tier**. Bulky, rarely-queried logs go to the **Data lake tier** only.
2. **How long hot.** A Sentinel-enabled workspace gets ==@90 days of analytics retention free==. Beyond that, up to 2 years, is billed pro rata.
3. **How long in total.** Set **total retention** up to 12 years for compliance. Days past analytics retention sit in the data lake tier at low cost.
4. **Who may change it.** Changing a tier or retention needs **Microsoft Sentinel Contributor + Log Analytics Contributor** on the workspace. In the Defender portal's unified RBAC it is **Data (manage)** under *Data operations*. Viewing needs Log Analytics Reader, or unified RBAC **Security data basics (read)**.

## What actually matters

| Concept | Azure Monitor / Log Analytics term | Defender portal (Sentinel) term | Default / limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hot, fully queryable state | **Analytics retention** (older docs: *interactive retention*) | **Analytics retention** in the **Analytics tier** | 30 days plain workspace; ==@90 days free== with Sentinel; max **2 years (730 days)** |
| Cheap state after hot period | **Long-term retention** (older docs: *archive*) | Data mirrored to the **Data lake tier** | Days between analytics and total retention |
| Whole lifetime | **Total retention** | **Total retention** | Max **12 years (4,383 days)** |
| Cheapest ingest, slow query | **Auxiliary** plan | **Data lake tier** (lake-only) | 30 days to 12 years |
| Cheap ingest, 30-day query window | **Basic** plan | (no separate Defender-portal tier — ==!manage Basic tables in the Log Analytics workspace, not the Defender portal==) | Query 30 days, total up to 12 years |
| Defender XDR hunting data | — | **XDR default tier** (advanced hunting) | 30 days free; 31–90 days in analytics = ingestion cost, no storage cost |
| Get old data back for full KQL | **Restore** → `_RST` table | Search page → Restore | Analytics and Basic only; min 2 days of data per restore |
| Find rows in old data | **Search job** → `_SRCH` table | Search page | All plans, incl. Auxiliary |

- **Shortening total retention** is safe for 30 days: Azure waits ==30 days before deleting==, so you can undo a mistake. Lengthening applies to data not yet removed. A change to analytics retention takes effect immediately.
- **Moving a table to the Data lake tier** stops analytics rules, custom detections and advanced hunting on it. Some XDR and solution tables cannot move.
- **Free ingestion is not free storage.** `AzureActivity` and `OfficeActivity` ingest free; storage beyond 90 days is billed.
- **Data lake onboarding** is once per tenant. It needs the primary Sentinel workspace connected to the Defender portal and a **direct subscription Owner** for billing. All Defender-connected workspaces in that region attach automatically; you cannot offboard one yourself. Afterwards, Auxiliary tables leave advanced hunting and are read through data lake KQL.

> **Two Microsoft sources disagree on the default.** The *Manage data* article says analytics data is kept "for 30 days by default", extendable "to 90 days for free" for Sentinel solution tables. Its own comparison table, the billing article and the Azure Monitor table-plans article say **90 days** for Sentinel. Learn it as: Sentinel-enabled workspace = **90 days free**; plain workspace and XDR hunting data = **30 days**.

> **The training unit is out of date.** Unit 3 teaches an "**Archive tier**" and says Basic tables keep "**eight days**" interactive. Product docs say Basic has a **30-day** query window, and the cheap state is **long-term retention** in the **data lake tier**. The product docs win.

## Where it lives

- **Defender portal (current home):** `Microsoft Defender portal > Microsoft Sentinel > Configuration > Tables` → select a table → **Manage table**. Set **Analytics retention** (30 days to 2 years) and **Total retention** (up to 12 years), or pick **Data lake tier** with one **Retention** value. Also on each connector's page under **Table management**.
- **Azure portal:** `Log Analytics workspaces > (your workspace) > Settings > Tables` → `...` → **Manage table**. Workspace default: `Log Analytics workspaces > (your workspace) > Usage and estimated costs > Data Retention`.
- Sentinel in the Azure portal is supported only until **March 31, 2027**. After that it is Defender portal only.

```bash
# Workspace default analytics retention (applies to tables without an override)
az monitor log-analytics workspace update -g rg-sec -n law-sentinel --retention-time 90

# One table: 1 year hot, 7 years total (2,555 days)
az monitor log-analytics workspace table update -g rg-sec --workspace-name law-sentinel \
  -n SecurityEvent --retention-time 365 --total-retention-time 2555
```

## Exam traps

- ==!"Archive tier" as a current name.== Today it is **long-term retention** in the **data lake tier**.
- ==!Analytics retention max is 12 years.== No. Analytics max is **2 years**. **Total** retention max is **12 years**.
- ==!Lowering retention deletes data at once.== Shortening **total** retention has a **30-day** wait before deletion.
- ==!Restore works on every table.== **Auxiliary/lake-only tables cannot be restored.** Use a search job or a KQL job.
- ==!Sentinel Contributor alone can change retention.== Changing a table needs **Log Analytics Contributor** as well, or unified RBAC **Data (manage)**.
- Compliance scenario: "query fast for 1 year, keep 7 years" → ==+analytics retention 365, total retention 2,555==.

## Skip

- **Skip:** the ingestion-health charts in *Manage data* (latency, volume, fluctuation thresholds). Health monitoring, not retention.
- **Skip:** data lake billing meters, the 2 TB / 12-hour minimum restore charge and per-GB prices. Not exam content.
- **Skip:** Spark notebooks and the notebook UI. Know only that KQL jobs, notebooks and search jobs read the lake.
- **Skip:** the PCI-DSS / SOX story in unit 3. Keep one rule: a table under two requirements gets the stricter one.
